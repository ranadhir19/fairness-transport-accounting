## 1. What is being ported (family 1, frozen specification)

From `upgrade_preregistration.md` WS3 and `upgrade_report.md`:

- **T1 (transport bound).** For any populations s, s′ and any state-blind
  threshold rule with alarm score S: |FAR_s(θ) − FAR_{s′}(θ)| ≤ TV(P_s^0,
  P_{s′}^0), where P_s^0 is the distribution of S over no-breach-within-H
  steps (the null) in s. Classical; no novelty claimed for the inequality.
- **T4 (empirical accounting protocol).** Per headline cell and model class,
  for each leave-one-population-out fold of the control forecaster (CTRL):
  TV between the test population's null step-score distribution and the
  pooled training null step-score distribution (50-bin histograms on [0,1],
  the paper's TV convention; 20-bin sensitivity), against the achieved
  |FAR_fold − FAR_train@θ|.
- **Family-1 accounting criterion (fixed there; ported verbatim in §8):**
  the theorem is said to *account for* the observed transport failure iff
  median fold TV ≥ 0.05 **and** T1's inequality holds on ≥ 99% of folds up
  to a histogram-estimation slack of 0.02.
- **Family-1 outcome (the result under test):** LR scope — median TV50
  0.3481, 50/50 folds within slack, criterion **MET**. GB scope — median
  TV50 0.1358, 50/51 folds (0.9804), criterion **NOT met** (one fold).
  Source: `upgrade_tester_report.md` §5 (independent recompute).

## 2. The second family — choice and justification

**Family 2 is UK Police stop-and-search** (data.police.uk, the official
police data portal for England & Wales): every stop-and-search record of the
candidate territorial forces for calendar 2024, with a frozen arrest-risk
score monitored for fairness gaps across ethnic groups, force by force,
month by month.

*Why this family satisfies the audit's criteria.* (i) **Different
data-generating process:** UK police administrative encounter records,
generated by operational policing decisions and statutory recording duties —
not a household survey (family 1 is US Census ACS PUMS microdata via
folktables). (ii) **Different protected-attribute structure:** UK self-defined
ethnicity uses the 18-category census classification (White British / Irish /
Gypsy or Irish Traveller / other White; four Mixed categories; five Asian;
three Black; other ethnic group), recorded alongside a *separate*
officer-defined 6-category coding — a dual-coded, multi-category structure,
unlike family 1's single US race code (RAC1P) binarised as White-alone vs
other. The primary grouping (§5) is White (any White self-defined category)
vs non-White; the full multi-category distribution is reported in the audit,
and officer-defined ethnicity is a registered sensitivity grouping. (iii) **A
genuine deployment-monitoring structure:** territorial police forces are
literal deployment units; stop-and-search fairness across ethnic groups is
an actively monitored quantity in UK policy; a risk score's alarm budget
transporting across forces is the same operational question as family 1's
transport across states. (iv) **Feasibility under the standing constraints:**
public, no key, small per-(force, month) files (0.1–1.1 MB JSON), cached
locally; total acquisition ≈ 60–70 MB across ~156 small files.

*Alternatives considered and rejected (recorded so the choice is auditable).*
Law School Admission: cross-sectional, a handful of school tiers, no time
axis — a monitoring accounting is undefined there, not merely weaker. COMPAS
(ProPublica): one county, ≈7k analysis rows — cannot support a
populations×steps design at anything like the registered batch scale.
MEPS: public-use files expose only 4 census regions as populations (4 folds)
and the raw consolidated files are large. HMDA: structurally ideal
(state×year lending) but per-state-year files are far beyond the
no-big-downloads constraint. Re-using another ACS task or ACS years is not a
second family (same DGP, same instrument).

## 4. Analysis populations

**Usable record:** `involved_person == true`, `self_defined_ethnicity`
present and non-empty, `outcome` present and non-empty. The population of
(force, month) is its usable records. All exclusion counts are reported per
force in the population audit.

**Label:** y = 1 iff `outcome == "Arrest"`.

**Protected attribute (primary):** A = 1 iff `self_defined_ethnicity` starts
with "White"; else A = 0. (Family 1: A = 1 iff RAC1P == 1, White alone. The
UK "White" umbrella is broader; the difference is disclosed wherever the two
families are compared, and the multi-category shares are reported.)
**Sensitivity grouping (registered):** officer-defined ethnicity, A = 1 iff
`officer_defined_ethnicity == "White"` — population audit only (shares and
population gaps), not a second accounting.

**Deployed-score features** (ethnicity — self-defined *and* officer-defined —
excluded, mirroring family 1's dropping of RAC1P from the deployed model's
features): search `type`; `object_of_search`; `legislation`; `age_range`
(missing → "Missing"); `gender` (missing → "Missing"); hour band of
`datetime` (00–05, 06–11, 12–17, 18–23; unparseable → "Missing");
`operation` (bool); `removal_of_more_than_outer_clothing` (bool). All
one-hot encoded. No other fields are used as features; in particular
`outcome_linked_to_object_of_search` and `location` are never features.

## 5. Inclusion rule (frozen; applied to acquired data before any gap is read)

A candidate force enters the **analysis set** iff:
(i) all 12 monthly files present and non-empty;
(ii) median monthly usable population ≥ 300;
(iii) minimum monthly usable population ≥ 150;
(iv) SDE-present share among involved-person records ≥ 0.80 overall;
(v) non-White share of usable records overall ∈ [0.04, 0.96];
(vi) arrest base rate overall ∈ [0.03, 0.60].
Every candidate's disposition and the numbers behind it are reported in the
population audit, pass or fail.

## 6. Deployed models and the monitor (ported protocol)

**Deployed models** — trained once on pooled **base-month (2024-01)** usable
records of the included forces (70/30 split, `np.random.default_rng(0)` —
family 1's construction), then frozen and applied to every (force, month)
population:
- **Primary (gating scope): logistic regression**, C = 1.0, lbfgs,
  max_iter = 2000, on the one-hot design (family 1's deployed class).
- **Secondary (reported, non-gating): gradient boosting**,
  `GradientBoostingClassifier(n_estimators=100, max_depth=3,
  learning_rate=0.1, random_state=0)` on the same design. (Family 1's GB
  implementation sits in the absent `upgrade/` tree; the *class* is what is
  mirrored, and this estimator choice is a disclosed family-2 decision.)

**Population metrics:** per (force, month), on the full usable population:
EO gap and DP gap of the deployed scores at the family-1 decision cut 0.5
(the `eo_gap` / `dp_gap` definitions of family 1's `common.py`, ported), base
rate, group shares. Saved as per-(force, month) score files plus a
population-metrics table — the family-1 artifact pattern.

**Monitor (ported from family 1's `monitor.py`):** trajectories = forces;
steps = months 2024-02…2024-12 (base month 2024-01 is the deployment month;
its rows open each series' feature history exactly as family 1's base year
did). Per (force, seed, month): one batch of **BATCH_N2 = 200** usable
records drawn without replacement (`np.random.default_rng(stable_seed("e2",
model, force, month, seed))`; whole population if usable n < 200), **25
seeds** (family 1's N_SEEDS). Batch features are the exact ports of family
1's monitor row: m_eo, m_dp, se_eo, se_dp, score_gap, share_a1, prev_diff,
eps_dp (score-TV drift summed over groups), eps_eo (summed over group×label
cells), m/m-lag/diff/slope per metric, d_share_a1, d_prev_diff — computed
with family 1's definitions and its 50-bin histogram TV on [0,1]. The one
scale change vs family 1 (batch 200 vs 2000) is forced by population sizes
(§2) and is disclosed wherever the families are compared.

**Labels (ported):** breach month per (force, metric, τ) = first month of
2024 whose *population* gap ≥ τ (population gaps computed over all 12
months, base included — family 1's construction). An instance at step t
(month index) has label_H = 1 iff pop_gap(t) < τ, t + H ≤ 2024-12, and the
breach month lies in (t, t+H]; label_H = 0 iff pop_gap(t) < τ, t + H ≤
2024-12, and no breach month lies in (t, t+H]; otherwise undefined
(excluded). Instances exist at steps Feb…Dec with defined labels.

**Forecaster (CTRL, ported from family 1's `forecast.py` "proposed"):**
per (metric, τ, H) cell, leave-one-force-out folds over the analysis set:
instances = monitor rows with defined labels; features = family 1's FEATS
lists verbatim (eo: m_eo, m_eo_lag1, d_eo, slope_eo, eps_eo, score_gap,
prev_diff, d_prev_diff, share_a1, d_share_a1; dp analogous), NaN → 0; model
= `CalibratedClassifierCV(pipeline(StandardScaler, LogisticRegression(C=1.0,
max_iter=2000)), method="sigmoid", cv=3)` fitted on the training forces'
instances. Scores p_tr (train instances), p_te (test instances). Last-value
scores computed exactly as family 1 (z = (τ − m)/σ, score = 1 − Φ(z)^H; σ =
SD of the training trajectories' population step-to-step gap changes,
floored at 1e-3). Candidate thresholds = the 99 unique quantiles (0.01…0.99)
of pooled [p_tr, plv_tr]; θ(target) = the candidate whose **train
step-FAR** (fraction of train *null* instances — label 0 — with score ≥ θ)
is closest to the target, ties toward the smaller FAR (family 1's
`pick_threshold`). FAR targets {0.05, 0.10, 0.20} are computed; the
accounting uses 0.10.

## 7. τ-selection rule (population audit only; the A1 precedent)

The headline cell is **(EO, τ\*, H = 2)**, where τ\* is fixed by this rule
applied to the §6 population audit — after the audit, **before any
forecaster or accounting computation** — and recorded in a dated amendment
appended to this file (exactly family 1's Amendment-A1 procedure):

τ\* = the smallest τ ∈ {0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20} such that
(a) ≥ 3 included forces record a first population EO crossing strictly after
    the base month (2024-01), **and**
(b) ≥ 40% of included forces have population EO gap < τ in the base month.

If no τ qualifies, τ\* = 0.05 and the headline cell is recorded as
vacuity-limited; the accounting is still run and reported, but E2 cannot
clear (§8). DP cells and τ ≠ τ\* EO cells are reported descriptively
(population audit and per-fold FAR spreads), never as alternative headline
cells. There is no cell-shopping: the headline cell is whatever this rule
produces.

## 8. The accounting endpoint and the E2 bar (binding)

**Accounting (ported T4):** on the headline cell, FAR target 0.10, per model
class, per LOSO fold: θ = the fold's CTRL θ(0.10);
FAR_fold = fraction of the test force's null instances with p_te ≥ θ;
FAR_train@θ = fraction of the pooled training null instances with p_tr ≥ θ;
TV50 = 50-bin histogram TV on [0,1] between the test force's null scores and
the pooled training null scores; TV20 likewise at 20 bins. A fold **passes**
iff |FAR_fold − FAR_train@θ| ≤ TV50 + 0.02. Reported per fold: null counts
(test/train), TV50, TV20, FAR_fold, FAR_train@θ, deviation, margin
(TV50 + 0.02 − deviation), pass/fail. Reported in summary: median TV50,
pass count/fraction, CTRL per-fold FAR spread (max − min) and fold-mean FAR
(the phenomenon's presence on family 2), the same summaries under TV20.

**Hypotheses.**
- H-E2-1 (the accounting travels): on the primary (LR) scope, median fold
  TV50 ≥ 0.05 and the fold pass fraction ≥ 0.99 — family 1's criterion,
  verbatim.
- H-E2-2 (the phenomenon is present): CTRL's per-fold achieved FAR at the
  0.10 target spreads materially on family 2 (max − min ≥ 0.10). Reported
  descriptively; if H-E2-2 fails *and* TVs are small, any "pass" is recorded
  as the accounting being untested rather than confirmed (see failure modes).

**E2 CLEARS iff all of:** (1) the τ rule (§7) yields a non-vacuous headline
cell (conditions (a) and (b) met at τ\*); (2) H-E2-1 holds on the LR scope;
(3) H-E2-2's caveat does not trigger (i.e., it is not the case that the
transport failure is absent and TVs are trivially small — operationalised
as: NOT (spread < 0.10 AND median TV50 < 0.05); note (2) already requires
median TV50 ≥ 0.05, so (3) binds exactly when spread < 0.10, in which case
the verdict is "accounting consistent but phenomenon weak on family 2" and
E2 clears only in that qualified form, stated as such).
The GB scope is computed and reported under the identical criterion but
does not gate — mirroring family 1's per-class adjudication (criterion met
on LR, missed by one fold on GB, credited *with that qualification printed*).
If GB's pass fraction < 0.90, the qualification is carried into the E2
verdict line itself.

**Failure modes (all are reportable outcomes; none is absorbed):**
- **F1 (bound does not price family 2):** fold TVs small while FAR
  deviations large, or ≥ 2 folds failing beyond slack → the null-score TV
  does *not* account for transport on family 2; the family-1 accounting is
  a single-family result.
- **F2 (nothing to price):** deviations ≈ 0 and TVs small → family 2 shows
  no material transport failure; the accounting is untested here, not
  falsified.
- **F3 (vacuity):** the τ rule falls back (§7) → the monitoring design
  produces no forecastable headline cell on this family at any registered
  τ; E2 fails on design-vacuity grounds.
Each failure mode's consequence for the paper's Level-1 verdict is stated in
`E2_RESULTS.md` in the audit's own terms (bound survives / collapses to a
single-benchmark observation / untested).

## 9. Determinism, checkpointing, validation

- `stable_seed(*parts)` = CRC32 of the "|"-joined string parts, masked to
  32 bits (family 1's function, ported). All stochastic steps are seeded;
  no wall-clock or hash-order dependence.
- Every stage writes artifacts under `elevation/` and **skips work whose
  artifacts already exist** (family-1 resumability), so a host reboot costs
  at most the in-flight stage.
- `elevation/validate_e2.py` independently recomputes, from the saved
  artifacts only (no builder code imported): a sample of population metrics
  from the saved score files; the full accounting (fold TVs, FARs, margins,
  criterion) from the saved step-instance scores; artifact completeness
  (all (force, month) cells, monitor rows, instance rows). Exit 0 only if
  every check passes. The E2 verdict in `E2_RESULTS.md` may rest only on
  numbers that this validation reproduces.

## 10. Scope limits (binding)

E2 computes no forecasting-skill claim (CTRL is the accounting's instrument,
exactly as family 1's T4 used it; FALSIFIER-U is not re-run and the falsified
forecasting claim is not re-tested). E2 edits no manuscript file, chooses no
venue, and contacts no journal. If E2 clears, E3 (theory) is unlocked under
its own frozen design note; if E2 does not clear, E3 does not start.
